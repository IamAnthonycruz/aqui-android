Aqui!

Texts the people you pick when you stay in one place for a while, so they know where you are without you having to tell them.

Download for Android

Open source on GitHub

How it works

1

Add your people

Mom after 30 minutes, a friend after 10. Each person gets their own timer and their own message.

2

Press start

Aqui! checks your spot about once a minute. Stay within about 150 m and the clock keeps counting.

3

They get a text

When someone's timer is up, they get one text from your number: the address and a map link.

Private by design

No account, no server

Texts go straight from your phone, from your own number.

No internet permission

Your location only leaves the phone inside the texts you set up.

Stays on your phone

Its small log cleans itself up: location fixes after 7 days, text history after 90. Uninstall and it's all gone.

Install

  1. Tap Download on this page, on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file. Android asks to allow installs from your browser: allow it, then tap Install. Google Play Protect may say it doesn't recognize the app. Tap Install anyway; Aqui! isn't on the Play Store.
  3. Open Aqui! and follow the setup pages. For location, choose Allow all the time.

Aqui! doesn't check for updates by itself (it has no internet access). Come back here for new versions and install them over the old one: your people and settings stay.

What you need

Android10 or newer
A phone plan with textingTexts are normal SMS from your number, at your carrier's usual rates. Wi-Fi-only tablets can't send them.
Google Play servicesMost Android phones have them. Phones without them (for example Huawei) can't get location.
Location onWi-Fi and cell location is enough. GPS isn't needed.
SpaceAbout 3 MB

Not an emergency service

Aqui! is a convenience, not a safety guarantee. A text can be late or never sent: no signal, a dead battery, a phone that stops apps in the background, or a location that's off by a few hundred meters. In an emergency, call 911. Only install it on your own phone. Aqui! is provided as is, with no warranty.
